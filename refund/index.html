Legal

Two guarantees, plainly stated

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Paying €249 to someone you have never met takes a leap of faith. This page exists so you do not have to take it. Come to a live class free before you decide anything — and once enrolled, you are covered twice over.

Before you pay anything

You can attend a live class free of charge, with no card and no commitment, before enrolling. The classes are the part of the programme you cannot judge from a sales page, so sit in on one and see for yourself. Nothing below matters if you use this first.

The 14-day money-back guarantee

  1. Enrol, come to the classes, and go through as much of the course as you like.
  2. If, within 14 days of the programme starting, you decide it is not for you, email contact@delfmasterclass.com from the address you enrolled with.
  3. You get a full refund and your place ends.
You do not need to justify yourself. "It's not what I expected" is a complete and sufficient reason. There is no form to fill in and no one will try to talk you out of it.

The pass guarantee

Separately, and for as long as you took part properly: if you sit the DELF B1 and do not pass, you join the next programme free of charge. To qualify you must have attended at least 70% of the live classes, submitted both written assignments by their deadlines, sat the exam within six months of the programme ending, and be able to show us your official result.

The pass guarantee gives you a free place on a future programme rather than your money back — the two guarantees do different jobs. It can be claimed once, and it is not transferable.

Why there are conditions. The teaching and the practice are ours to stand behind. Whether you do the work is not, and a guarantee that ignored that would be a marketing line rather than a promise.

How long a refund takes

Refunds are issued through Payhip, which handled your payment. We process the request within two working days. After that, the money takes a few more days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer — that part is out of our hands.

The refund covers the full amount you paid, including any VAT. If you chose the three-instalment option, any remaining instalments are cancelled.

The one exception

Both guarantees cover honest use. If someone enrols, downloads every PDF and audio file, and asks for a refund on day 14 having clearly taken the material with no intention of taking part, we reserve the right to decline. The same applies to anyone re-enrolling repeatedly to claim a guarantee more than once. These are rare cases and not the kind of thing we look for — but they need saying.

Private 1-to-1 sessions

Private sessions are bought separately from the programme, and work differently because they reserve a slot of real time:

  • Cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice — no cost, and you can rebook whenever suits you.
  • Cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, or miss the session without telling us, and the session cannot be refunded.
  • If we ever have to cancel on you, you get the choice between a new slot and a full refund.

Your statutory rights

These guarantees are offered voluntarily and sit on top of your rights as a consumer, not instead of them. Nothing here reduces the protection the law gives you. For the legal detail on the right of withdrawal for digital content, see the Terms of Sale.

Questions before you enrol

If you are unsure whether the programme fits your level, your exam date or your time zone, ask before enrolling rather than after — email contact@delfmasterclass.com and you will get an honest answer, including "this probably is not right for you" if that is the truth.
