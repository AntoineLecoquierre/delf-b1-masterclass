Refund policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Short and blunt: all sales are final. Below is what that means, what happens to coaching sessions you have not used, and what to read before you decide.

Can I get a refund?

No. The DELF B1 Masterclass is a digital product and all sales are final. Once you receive access, your purchase cannot be cancelled or refunded. The same applies to every coaching package: sessions reserve real time in a real calendar, time that is not offered to anyone else.

By purchasing, you accept our Terms of Sale, including this no-refund policy. At checkout you also confirm that you want immediate access and that you understand you are giving up the right to withdraw — worth reading before you tick it.

This does not affect any rights or refunds required by applicable law. Consumer rules differ from country to country, and some cannot be signed away by anything written here — particularly over coaching sessions not yet delivered. Where the law where you live gives you a right we have not validly excluded, that right stands whatever this page says. If you think that applies to you, write to us and say so; we would rather sort it out directly than have you go through your card issuer.

Find out before you pay, not after

Because there is no way back, the free material is not a marketing afterthought — it is the trial.

The DELF B1 Cheat Sheet is four pages, at no charge and with no card. Same material, same way of explaining things as the course. If the way I explain the exam does not click, you will know within ten minutes of reading, and you will have paid nothing to find out.

Coaching sessions you have not used

Sessions are not refundable, but they do not vanish either:

  • Every session in a package is valid for four months from the date of purchase.
  • Cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice — no cost, and the session goes back in your balance.
  • Cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, or simply not turn up, and that session is used.
  • If we ever have to cancel on you, the session goes back in your balance and the four months are extended by the delay.

You can also move up to a larger package at any point in those four months by paying the difference in price. Sessions already used are deducted, and the four months still run from your first purchase. It is the one way to change your mind after buying — you never get money back, but you are never stuck either.

If something serious happens — illness, a bereavement, your exam date moving — write to contact@delfmasterclass.com. We cannot refund, but we can almost always extend.

Questions before you buy

If you are unsure whether the course fits your level or your exam date, ask before buying rather than after — email contact@delfmasterclass.com and you will get an honest answer, including "this probably is not right for you" if that is the truth.
