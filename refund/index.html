Cancel anytime, and a 14-day guarantee

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Signing up to a monthly payment with someone you have never met takes a leap of faith. This policy exists so you do not have to take it. There is no minimum term, you can stop whenever you like, and your first month is guaranteed.

Cancelling

There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee. You can stop your membership at any time, in one click from your Payhip account, or by emailing contact@delfmasterclass.com and asking us to do it for you.

Cancelling stops the next payment. You keep full access — live sessions and course — until the end of the month you have already paid for. Nothing is cut off early, and nothing is charged afterwards.

Most people leave, and that is fine. The DELF B1 is a finite goal. Members typically stay the two or three months their preparation takes, sit the exam, and cancel. That is exactly how this is meant to work — you will never get an email trying to talk you out of it.

The 14-day guarantee on your first month

Cancelling stops future payments, but it does not refund the month in progress. So that joining carries no risk at all, your first month is separately guaranteed:

  1. Join, attend the live sessions, and go through as much of the course as you like.
  2. If, within 14 days of joining, you decide it is not for you, email contact@delfmasterclass.com from the address you signed up with.
  3. You get that first payment back in full, and your membership ends.
You do not need to justify yourself. "It's not what I expected" is a complete and sufficient reason. There is no form to fill in and no one will try to talk you out of it.

How long it takes

Refunds are issued through Payhip, which handles the billing. We process the request within two working days. After that, the money takes a few more days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer — that part is out of our hands.

The refund covers the full amount of that first payment, including any VAT.

The one exception

The guarantee covers honest use. If someone joins, downloads every PDF and audio file, and asks for a refund on day 14 having clearly taken the material with no intention of taking part, we reserve the right to decline. The same applies to anyone rejoining repeatedly to claim the guarantee more than once. These are rare cases and not the kind of thing we look for — but they need saying.

Private 1-to-1 sessions

Private sessions are bought separately from the membership, and work differently because they reserve a slot of real time:

  • Cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice — no cost, and you can rebook whenever suits you.
  • Cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, or miss the session without telling us, and the session cannot be refunded.
  • If we ever have to cancel on you, you get the choice between a new slot and a full refund.

Your statutory rights

This guarantee is offered voluntarily and sits on top of your rights as a consumer, not instead of them. Nothing here reduces the protection the law gives you. For the legal detail on the right of withdrawal for digital content, see the Terms of Sale.

Questions before you buy

If you are unsure whether the membership fits your level, your exam date or your time zone, ask before joining rather than after — email contact@delfmasterclass.com and you will get an honest answer, including "this probably is not right for you" if that is the truth.
