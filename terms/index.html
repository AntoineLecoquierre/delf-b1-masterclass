Terms of Sale

Last updated: 22 August 2026

These terms apply to the DELF B1 Pass Programme and to any private 1-to-1 session you book. They are deliberately short and in plain English.

Who you are buying from

The DELF B1 Masterclass is created and taught by Antoine L., an independent French teacher based in Paris. Contact: contact@delfmasterclass.com.

The programme is sold through Payhip, which acts as the seller of record. Payhip takes the payment, issues your receipt and handles VAT. Your contract for the purchase is therefore concluded with Payhip, while the teaching and the course content come from us.

What the programme includes

The DELF B1 Pass Programme runs for eight weeks. Over that period you get:

  • Four live group classes every week, held online and taught in English by Antoine L. You may attend as many or as few as you wish.
  • The complete self-paced course — video modules, PDF workbooks and audio files covering all four sections of the exam.
  • Two written assignments marked against the official DELF scoring grid and returned with the score they would have earned.
  • One full mock exam in the final fortnight, returned with a predicted score.

From time to time a class may be moved or cancelled — for illness or a public holiday, for example. When that happens you will be told as early as possible, and the class is made up or replaced within the programme. Because there are four each week, an occasional cancellation does not materially reduce what you paid for and does not by itself entitle you to a refund.

Group classes are exactly that: group classes. Numbers vary and you are not guaranteed individual attention within them. If you want the exam rehearsed privately, book a 1-to-1 session.

Marking turnaround for assignments and the mock exam is normally a few working days. Assignments must be submitted by the deadlines given during the programme; work sent in after the programme ends cannot be marked.

The pass guarantee

If you take part properly and still do not pass, you join the next programme free of charge. To qualify you must:

  • attend at least 70% of the live classes;
  • submit both written assignments by their deadlines;
  • sit the DELF B1 within six months of your programme ending; and
  • send us your official result showing you did not pass.

The guarantee gives you a free place on a future programme. It is not a cash refund, it cannot be exchanged for one, and it is not transferable to another person. It can be claimed once per person.

To be plain about why the conditions exist: what we can stand behind is the teaching and the practice. We cannot promise a result for work that was never done.

Payment

The programme is a one-off purchase, not a subscription. Nothing recurs and there is nothing to cancel. If you choose the three-instalment option, the two remaining payments are taken automatically on the same date in each of the following two months; a missed instalment may suspend access until the account is up to date.

After the programme

When the eight weeks end, the live classes and access to the online course close. There is nothing further to pay. The PDF workbooks and audio files are downloadable, and anything you downloaded while enrolled is yours to keep.

Price and VAT

Prices shown on this site are in euros and exclude VAT. Payhip calculates any tax due based on your billing country and shows the final total before you confirm payment. The amount you see at checkout is the amount you pay.

Delivery

Access is granted immediately after your payment is confirmed. You receive an email from Payhip with your access link, the course material and the joining details for the live classes. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then write to contact@delfmasterclass.com and we will sort it out.

What you may and may not do with the course

Enrolling gives you a personal, non-transferable licence to use the course materials and to attend the live classes for your own learning. You may download the PDFs and audio files and keep them.

You may not share your access or your joining link, redistribute the files, republish the content, resell it, or use it to teach paying students of your own. Live classes may not be recorded or streamed by participants. The materials remain our intellectual property.

Your right of withdrawal

Under EU consumer law you normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content delivered immediately, that right lapses once you agree to instant access and acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal — which is what you confirm at checkout.

We go further anyway. Regardless of the above, we offer a voluntary 14-day money-back guarantee. See the Refund Policy for how it works.

1-to-1 sessions

Private 1-to-1 sessions are optional, booked through the scheduling link on the site and paid separately from the programme. If you need to move one, give at least 24 hours' notice and it will be rescheduled at no cost. Sessions missed without notice cannot be refunded.

What we do and do not promise

The programme teaches you the format, the scoring and the strategies of the DELF B1, and gives you regular live practice, all built on real exam experience. It is designed to give you the best possible preparation.

It cannot, however, guarantee that you will pass — your result depends on your own work, your starting level and your performance on the day. We are not affiliated with France Éducation international or any official DELF examination centre, and we do not deliver the DELF diploma.

If something goes wrong

Write to contact@delfmasterclass.com first — nearly everything is solved that way. Nothing in these terms limits the statutory rights you have as a consumer.

Changes

These terms may be updated; the date at the top of the page reflects the current version. The terms that apply to your programme are the ones published on the day you enrolled.
