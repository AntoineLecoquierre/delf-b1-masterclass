Last updated: 1 September 2026

These terms apply when you buy the DELF B1 Masterclass, and to any private 1-to-1 session you book. They are deliberately short and in plain English.

Who you are buying from

The DELF B1 Masterclass is created and taught by Antoine L., a native French teacher. The business selling it is established in Indonesia. Contact: contact@delfmasterclass.com.

The course is sold through Payhip, which acts as the seller of record. Payhip takes the payment, issues your receipt and handles VAT. Your contract for the purchase is therefore concluded with Payhip, while the course content comes from us.

What you get

The DELF B1 Masterclass is a self-paced online course. There is no timetable and nothing live to attend. It consists of:

  • Five video modules covering the exam format and all four sections, explained in English.
  • Seven PDF workbooks — two full listening exams, two reading tests, two writing prompts with graded model answers, a speaking pack and a grammar reference, with the official scoring grids decoded.
  • Six audio recordings for the listening practice.
  • A six-week study plan.

The course may be improved over time; improvements are included at no extra cost. Nothing here is marked or reviewed individually — the model answers and annotated keys are there so you can correct your own work.

You keep access for as long as the course exists, with no recurring fee and no expiry date on your side. It does not mean the course is guaranteed to exist forever. If it were ever permanently withdrawn you would be told in advance and given the chance to download everything. The PDFs and audio files are downloadable, so anything you save is yours whatever happens.

The course is a one-off purchase, not a subscription. Nothing recurs and there is nothing to cancel.

Coaching packages

Packages combine the method with a number of private 50-minute sessions. The method is delivered immediately; the sessions are booked by you, whenever suits you, through the scheduling link.

Sessions in the two smaller packages are run by a qualified teacher — Antoine, or a teacher working with him. The largest package is taught by Antoine personally. Every session in a package is valid for four months from the date of purchase. Rescheduling needs 24 hours' notice, as below.

There is no charge for the change itself, and moving down or converting a package back to money is not possible.

1-to-1 sessions

Private 1-to-1 sessions are optional, booked through the scheduling link on the site and paid separately from the course. If you need to move one, give at least 24 hours' notice and it will be rescheduled at no cost. Sessions missed without notice cannot be refunded.

The course teaches you the format, the scoring and the strategies of the DELF B1, built on real exam experience. It is designed to give you the best possible preparation.

It cannot, however, guarantee that you will pass — your result depends on your own work, your starting level and your performance on the day. We are not affiliated with France Éducation international or any official DELF examination centre, and we do not deliver the DELF diploma.
