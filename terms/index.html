Terms of Sale

Last updated: 22 August 2026

These terms apply to your DELF B1 Masterclass membership and to any 1-to-1 session you book. They are deliberately short and in plain English.

Who you are buying from

The DELF B1 Masterclass is created and taught by Antoine L., an independent French teacher based in Paris. Contact: contact@delfmasterclass.com.

The membership is sold through Payhip, which acts as the seller of record. Payhip takes the payment, manages the recurring billing, issues your receipts and handles VAT. Your contract for the purchase is therefore concluded with Payhip, while the teaching and the course content come from us.

What your membership includes

A DELF B1 Masterclass membership gives you, for as long as it is active:

  • Four live group sessions every week, held online and taught in English by Antoine L. You may attend as many or as few as you wish.
  • The complete self-paced course — video modules, PDF workbooks and audio files covering all four sections of the exam.
  • Anything added to the course while your membership is running, at no extra cost.

Sessions run every week of the year. From time to time a session may be moved or cancelled — for illness or a public holiday, for example. When that happens you will be told as early as possible. Because there are four sessions a week, the occasional cancellation does not materially reduce what you are paying for, and does not by itself entitle you to a refund.

Group sessions are exactly that: group sessions. The number of participants varies, and you are not guaranteed individual attention within them. If you want the exam rehearsed privately, book a 1-to-1 session.

Billing, renewal and cancellation

The membership is a monthly subscription. You are charged when you join and then once a month on the same date, automatically, until you cancel. There is no minimum term.

You can cancel at any time, from your Payhip account or by emailing us. Cancelling stops the next payment; it does not refund the month already in progress. You keep full access until the end of the period you have paid for, and access ends when that period does.

If a payment fails, we may retry it and will let you know. If it cannot be collected, access is suspended until the account is up to date.

We may change the price of the membership. If we do, existing members are told at least 30 days in advance, the change never applies to a period already paid for, and you are free to cancel before it takes effect.

What happens when you leave

When your membership ends, access to the live sessions and to the online course ends with it. This is what makes a modest monthly price possible instead of a large one-off fee.

The PDF workbooks and audio files are downloadable, and anything you downloaded while you were a member is yours to keep and to carry on using for your own preparation.

Price and VAT

Prices shown on this site are in euros and exclude VAT. Payhip calculates any tax due based on your billing country and shows the final total before you confirm payment. The amount you see at checkout is the amount you pay each month.

Delivery

Access is granted immediately after your first payment is confirmed. You receive an email from Payhip with your access link, the course material and the joining details for the live sessions. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then write to contact@delfmasterclass.com and we will sort it out.

What you may and may not do with the course

Your membership gives you a personal, non-transferable licence to use the course materials and to attend the live sessions for your own learning. You may download the PDFs and audio files and keep them.

You may not share your access or your joining link, redistribute the files, republish the content, resell it, or use it to teach paying students of your own. Live sessions may not be recorded or streamed by participants. The materials remain our intellectual property.

Your right of withdrawal

Under EU consumer law you normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content delivered immediately, that right lapses once you agree to instant access and acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal — which is what you confirm at checkout.

We go further anyway. Regardless of the above, we offer a voluntary 14-day money-back guarantee on your first month. See the Refund Policy for how it works.

1-to-1 sessions

Private 1-to-1 sessions are optional, booked through the scheduling link on the site and paid separately from the membership. If you need to move one, give at least 24 hours' notice and it will be rescheduled at no cost. Sessions missed without notice cannot be refunded.

What we do and do not promise

The membership teaches you the format, the scoring and the strategies of the DELF B1, and gives you regular live practice, all built on real exam experience. It is designed to give you the best possible preparation.

It cannot, however, guarantee that you will pass — your result depends on your own work, your starting level and your performance on the day. We are not affiliated with France Éducation international or any official DELF examination centre, and we do not deliver the DELF diploma.

If something goes wrong

Write to contact@delfmasterclass.com first — nearly everything is solved that way. Nothing in these terms limits the statutory rights you have as a consumer.

Changes

These terms may be updated; the date at the top of the page reflects the current version. For a subscription, the terms that apply are the ones published at the start of your current billing period, and material changes are announced at least 30 days ahead.
