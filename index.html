Why this one matters

This is not a French course. It is a door.

Almost nobody sits the DELF B1 for the pleasure of it. They sit it because a diploma is the last thing standing between them and something that has been waiting for years.

I

A residency file

The language certificate is the piece the préfecture asks for, and the one that sends people back to the queue when it is missing.

II

A university place

Programmes and scholarships that need proof of French — with an application deadline that does not move because you were not ready.

III

A life you already built

You live here. You work here. The paperwork simply has not caught up with the fact yet, and this is the last box left to tick.

Sitting the exam again costs the fee, and months. That is the real price of not being ready — and the reason this programme is built to get you across the line the first time.

The gap

You cannot learn to speak in silence.

Reading and listening you can train alone. But most candidates walk into the production orale having not spoken French aloud to another person all month — and it shows in the first thirty seconds.

The programme

Eight weeks, in order.

Not a library you are left alone with. A sequence, with four live classes every week running alongside it, ending the week you sit the exam.

Week 1Foundation

The exam blueprint

The four sections, how each is marked, and the rule that fails candidates with good overall scores. You leave week one knowing exactly what the examiner rewards — and with a day-by-day plan for the seven weeks that follow.

Weeks 2–3Comprehension

Listening & reading under pressure

The Two-Listen Strategy, then skim-then-scan for the written papers. Two complete listening exams with their audio, two reading tests, every answer key annotated so you see why the trap was a trap.

Weeks 4–5Production écrite

Writing that scores

More than half the marks here are for structure, not vocabulary. You get the four-paragraph frame that works for any prompt, the official grid decoded in English — and you write two essays that come back marked.
