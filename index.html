Why capable people fail

Your French is probably fine. Your preparation isn't.

I

Everything is in French

The official material explains the exam in the language you are still learning. You end up guessing at the instructions instead of learning the method.

II

Nobody shows you the grid

Examiners mark against fixed criteria. Almost no candidate has read them, so they spend their effort on the things that earn the fewest points.

III

One weak section sinks you

You pass at 50/100 — but score under 5/25 in any single section and you fail outright, however good the rest was. Most people learn this too late.
