Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data DELF B1 Masterclass collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read — not to be skimmed past.

Who is responsible for your data

DELF B1 Masterclass is operated by Antoine L., an independent French teacher. For any question about this policy or about your data, write to contact@delfmasterclass.com — that address reaches a real person, not a ticketing queue.

We never sell your data, and we do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits. It is switched off by default: no analytics cookie is written and no data is sent to Google until you press Accept on the banner. If you press Decline, or simply ignore the banner, analytics stays off and the site works exactly the same.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage under the key cookieConsent. To change your mind, clear your browser storage for this site and the banner will appear again.

No other cookies. This site sets no advertising, profiling or social-media cookies of any kind.

Services we rely on

Running the site means a few third parties necessarily see some data. Here is the complete list:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. It processes your IP address in its server logs, as any web host does.
  • Payhip — sells and delivers the programme. Payhip acts as the seller of record: it collects your payment details and billing country and handles VAT. Your card details never reach us — we only see that a payment was made, and your name and email.
  • Stripe — processes the payment on Payhip's behalf.
  • Google Analytics — usage statistics, only after you consent.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces used on this site are served by Google, which means your IP address is visible to Google when the page loads.
  • Google Calendar / Google Workspace — used to book 1-to-1 sessions and to receive email.
  • Google Meet — used to host the Saturday classes. Other participants see the name you join under; you can choose what that is.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Union. Where that is the case, transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep things

  • Analytics data: 14 months, then deleted automatically by Google.
  • Payment records: as long as accounting law requires — in France, ten years.
  • Assignments and mock exams you submit: kept while your programme runs and for six months afterwards, so a pass-guarantee claim can be checked. Deleted on request.
  • Emails: as long as our conversation is useful, then deleted.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive it in a portable format. You can also withdraw your consent to analytics at any time, which does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these, email contact@delfmasterclass.com. We will reply within one month. If you are not satisfied with the response, you may complain to your national data protection authority — in France, the CNIL.

Children

This course is aimed at adults and older teenagers preparing for the DELF B1. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 15. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be announced on the site.
